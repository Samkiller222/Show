How this works

Finalists are filled in from the booklet. Working and Terrier come from the Sunday evening results once their groups are judged on this device. Change any finalist with its dropdown. Then pick the winner (and runner-up where there is one) and tick Given.

If any of the six Best in Show finalists is a puppy, junior or veteran, Best in Show is judged before Best Puppy, Best Junior or Best Veteran in Show.
