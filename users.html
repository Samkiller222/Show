Roles

  • Admin Everything, plus this page: accounts, roles, and signing in as anyone.
  • Organiser Everything except users: shows, entries, dogs, breeds, judging days and awards.
  • Steward Records awards. Can see everything else but not change it.
  • Viewer Looks only. Can't change anything.
  • Owner Sees only the dogs linked to their email (set under Owner logins on each dog's page in Club dogs), enters them into Open shows, and sees and pays what their entries cost.
