What the statuses mean

  • Active: a paid-up member. With a "member until" date, it turns to Expired by itself the day after.
  • Pending: waiting to join or renew. New owner accounts start here.
  • Expired: the membership has lapsed.

The status is for the club's records and to let owners know where they stand. It doesn't stop anyone entering dogs or paying.
